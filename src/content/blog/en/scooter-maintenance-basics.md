Most e-scooter problems don't show up as failures — they show up as a creak, a wobble, or a suddenly shorter ride. Ten minutes a month catches almost all of them.

## 1. Tire pressure

Under-inflated tires kill your range and chew through tread. Check monthly with a standard bike pump. Most Joyor air tires run at **45–50 PSI**; check the sidewall on yours.

## 2. Brake bite

Squeeze both brakes while stationary. They should feel firm and engage within the first third of the lever travel. If the lever pulls all the way to the bar, it's time to adjust the cable or replace the pads.

## 3. Fold lock

The folding mechanism is the most-stressed joint on the scooter. Every month, check that the lock engages with a satisfying *click* and there's no play side-to-side when folded. Replace the locking lever if you feel any wobble.

## 4. Screws and stem

Two minutes with an Allen key. Check every visible bolt — especially the stem clamp, handlebar clamp, and deck bolts. Vibration slowly backs them out.

## 5. Battery habit

Don't store at 100% for weeks, don't let it drop to 0%, don't leave it in the freezing cold. That's it. Batteries that get basic respect last 3× longer than batteries that don't.
