The Joyor S10S-Z arrived in a box roughly the size of a small fridge. Twenty minutes and four Allen bolts later it was unfolded in my kitchen, powered on, and ready to go.

## The numbers

- **Dual 1000W motors** (2000W peak)
- **60V 18Ah battery**
- **~75 km** manufacturer range
- **11-inch all-terrain tires**
- **Hydraulic front suspension + spring rear**

## On city pavement

Acceleration from a standstill is immediate but never twitchy — the dual-motor controller ramps power smoothly even on wet stone. At the legal top speed the ride is calm and stable, and the hydraulic front fork soaks up tram tracks and drain covers without drama.

## On cobblestones

This is where most scooters start rattling your teeth. The S10S-Z's combination of 11-inch air tires and dual suspension turns a medieval street surface into something that merely feels textured. I rode a full 4 km of pre-war cobble without fatigue.

## Off the pavement

A bored Sunday afternoon took me onto a forest trail that I would normally walk. The all-terrain tires held grip through wet leaves and loose gravel. Dual motors make climbs effortless. This is not a mountain bike — don't jump it — but it handles trail duty with surprising composure.

## What I didn't love

Folded, it's still a heavy scooter. Carrying it up three flights of stairs is a workout. If your commute includes stairs, look at the F series instead.
